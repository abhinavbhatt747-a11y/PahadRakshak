💥 Critical Challenges in Mountain Regions

🏔️ Mountain Vulnerability
Narrow highways (NH-07, Kedarnath route) easily blocked by landslides, trapping tourists, pilgrims, & ambulances.
📞 Control Room Overload
Hundreds of simultaneous calls during cloudbursts. Control rooms lack automated risk ranking systems.
📱 Information Silos
Viral disaster videos & posts on X (Twitter) go unnoticed by official dispatchers.
📑 Static CRUD Portals
Existing projects are dumb databases. They don't calculate risk or prioritize life-threatening situations.

Product Workflow

⚙️ Closed-Loop Emergency Response Flow

1. Ingestion Citizen App + Social Media (X/Twitter) + Rainfall Telemetry
2. AI Scoring Normalized 0-100 Score + Explainable AI Reasons Output
3. Prioritization Smart Priority Queue auto-promotes Critical emergencies to top
4. Dispatch Authority assigns SDRF/NDRF team with route time estimation
5. Resolution Status update to RESOLVED + Citizen notification sent
Innovation Pillar 1

🤖 Multi-Factor AI Risk Engine & Explainable AI

6 Weighted Risk Inputs:
• Incident Severity
• Rainfall Telemetry (mm)
• Historical Hazard Zone
• Report Density
• Population Impact
• Road Blockage Access
Explainable AI Output Example:
Risk Score: 87/100 (CRITICAL RISK)
  • Heavy rainfall signal detected in zone (65mm precipitation).
  • 25 affected individuals reported at risk.
  • Road blockage confirmed — emergency response access obstructed.
  • 3 historical slope failure events recorded nearby.
