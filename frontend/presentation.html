PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Himalayan Mountain Disaster Crisis

🔴 Frequent Landslides & Cloudbursts

Heavy monsoons trigger slope failures on NH-07 (Badrinath) and NH-109 (Kedarnath), stranding thousands of pilgrims without warning.

🛑 Blind Commercial Navigation

Google Maps relies on passive traffic slowing and blindly routes vehicles into active landslide debris without mountain hazard alerts.

⏳ Delayed Emergency Triage

Distress calls lack precise hardware GPS coordinates and verified photos, delaying SDRF, NDRF, and BRO heavy machinery dispatch.

📢 Rumors & Ghost Blockages

Unverified social media rumors cause panic, while solved road clearance updates take hours to reach travelers on the road.

OUR SOLUTION

PahadRakshak: Intelligent Disaster Ecosystem

🔀

Conditional Dual-Route Bypass

Renders 1 clean blue line when safe; 2 distinct paths (Red Unsafe vs Green Safe Bypass) ONLY when primary route is blocked.

🏛️

Official Govt Live Sync

Scans and ingests live bulletins directly from UKSDMA, IMD Dehradun, BRO Shivalik, and Police PCR with 1-click source proof links.

🛡️

Dual-Portal Architecture

Frictionless Public Citizen Portal for travelers + Secured Authority Command Center with Officer Badge Verification (`UKSDMA-OFFICER-01`).

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

End-to-End Data Pipeline & AI GIS Engine

1. Data Ingestion
Citizen SOS + Hardware GPS + Live Crawlers (UKSDMA/IMD/BRO/Police)
2. AI Risk Engine
Multi-Factor Risk Score (0-100) + Spatial Clustering + Duplicate Detector
3. FastAPI Backend
Python FastAPI + SQLite/PostgreSQL + ResolutionChecker Background Feeder
4. Interactive Clients
Leaflet GIS Map + OSRM Driving Engine + Progressive Web App (PWA)

AI RISK CALCULATOR

Multi-Factor AI Risk Scoring (0-100)

Risk Score = (Severity × 25%) + (Rainfall × 20%) + (History × 15%) + (Road Blocked × 15%) + (Affected People × 15%) + (Density × 10%)
CRITICAL (80-100)
Cloudburst, Full Blockage, Life Threat
HIGH (60-79)
Landslide, Heavy Debris, Road Blocked
MODERATE (40-59)
Minimal Hazard, Traffic Slowed
LOW (0-39)
Minor Rain / Clear
SMART NAVIGATION

Conditional Dual-Route Bypass System

✅ SAFE HIGHWAY SCENARIO

When the primary highway is safe and clear of disasters, the map renders 1 single glowing blue line without screen clutter.

🔀 HAZARD SCENARIO (UNSAFE PRIMARY)

When a disaster or road blockage is detected on the primary highway, the engine renders 2 distinct paths:

  • Route A (Red Dashed Line): Unsafe Primary Highway with Road Blockage alert
  • Route B (Green Glowing Line): Recommended 100% Safe Bypass Route
GOVT SYNC & PURGE

Live Government Sync & Auto Map Purging

🏛️ Official Government Sync

Direct REST crawlers syncing UKSDMA, IMD Dehradun, BRO Shivalik, and Police PCR updates with 1-click source verification links pointing directly to official domains.

🧹 Auto-Clearance Map Purging

ResolutionCheckerService scans BRO heavy machinery clearance logs and purges resolved road blockages from live maps automatically (Zero Ghost Markers Policy).

LOCATION WEATHER

Location Weather Broadcasting & IMD Radar

📍 THIS IS YOUR LOCATION & THIS IS THE WEATHER IN YOUR AREA

Displays real-time weather forecasts (temperature, rain mm, wind speed, humidity) fetched directly for the user's exact hardware GPS coordinates alongside statewide IMD precipitation Doppler circles.
